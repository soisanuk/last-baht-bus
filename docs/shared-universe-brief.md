# Shared-universe lore brief

For a sister game in the Pattaya universe of *The Last Baht Bus*. Lore only; no gameplay link.

## 1. The White Rabbit line

**Premise.** Fast Eddy — "Rabbit" — keeps the White Rabbit, a small bar up in Naklua, north of the tourist town. Sober since a morning he woke somewhere he should not have, he is a man in two layers: the easy host on top, and under it a grudge against the White Dish Group (WDG), the foreign-fronted outfit whose money is buying up the soi. He wants it dead.

**Stages.** The player earns Rabbit's trust the slow way — nights, drinks, talk; nothing bought. Rabbit lets him into the plan: a crypto heist aimed at WDG's money, worked through a terminal step by step. The road forks rather than filters: the player can be the mule (an account in plain sight, Nont's trade) or the operator (hands on the keys). The heist misreads its target — what moves is not quite WDG's, and the people who notice are not the ones Rabbit expected. Heat from the Cyber Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) rises as a consequence of what was touched. A later landing can fall on Nont, the kid, because Rabbit used him; Rabbit's guilt, Tan's favour called early, or the player's own cash can clear him.

**Resolution.** Nobody is arrested on stage and nobody is shot. The ending is structural: the player is kept or dropped by the people whose position he changed, and the bar, the kid and the grudge land where his choices put them. The key is the *suay* inversion — bad luck read backwards as the town saying who was out of favour.

**Tone.** Grounded, dry, PG-13. Structural failure, never a morality tale. Status: Rabbit and his bar are built; the heist is designed in outline and partly unbuilt — canon in shape, not in detail.

## 2. People

- **Fast Eddy / Rabbit** — owner, the White Rabbit, Naklua. Easy host over a sober man with a grudge. Knows WDG from inside, the plan, and that he used the kid.
- **Tan** — ผู้กว้างขวาง, the broad man; mid-thirties; favours, never money; the hidden hub behind every arrival; walks the soi by the hour, home by midnight. Knows everyone's debt to him. His network profits from WDG's buying, so the heist cuts across his interest.
- **Nont** — the priced fixer, luk khrueng (half American): ฿200 a locate, five percent on cash moved, a mule account in plain sight. Knows how money moves and who holds it; the one the heist can land on.
- **Mort** — the Owl's columnist; Baltimore by passport, a British paper's register by forty-one years. Holds the town's rumours as leads, never proof.
- **Ryan Powers / WDG** — offstage founder and outfit. Entirely fictional.
- **CCIB** — an institution, never a face; appears as heat.

## 3. The Bangkok game (working title *Ground Floor*; name open)

**Premise.** A cyberpunk genre-shift, a few years on. The protagonist is luk khrueng, Pattaya-raised, a bridge between worlds, who knows Eddy and Tan. The spine: Eddy wants WDG dead, Tan's people profit from it, the player stands between.

**Setting.** Bangkok's scam economy, built on real research: the dead Pantip Plaza as a husk, call-centre fronts, mule and frozen accounts, the liability regime, the CCIB. Code-switching — Thai, English, register — is the texture.

**Factions.** The scam operators; the CCIB; Tan's patronage network; WDG's money; the ground-floor people who carry the accounts.

**Tone.** LBB's doctrine: grounded, structural, nobody moral-graded. It must work for a player who never saw LBB — LBB payoffs are bonuses, never keys.

## 4. Do not contradict

- Thai organised power is patronage, not families: the syndicate is Tan's favours; the Darkside is a *jao pho* family; the envelope is what you pay when you cannot be inside.
- Tan: favours never money; met standing at his parked car, never at the wheel; the quarter-turned glass is his alone. Nont: ฿200 and five percent, half American. Mort: Baltimore, forty-one years. Madam Oy: eight years in Hong Kong.
- A foreigner cannot legally stand behind his own bar; the paper by the till names the owner.
- Baht-bus fare ฿15. Pattaya faces west. Soi 6 shuts at two.
- No real families, names or allegations — structural pattern only. WDG/Powers and PLG/Laurent Vasseur are the fictional groups.
- Suicide is never a mechanic or an ending; violence is a threat never made. Declining is always free; face-loss is permanent; being told the truth is not a prize.
