# Essay ledger — public writing on the Pattaya bar economy as research material

Generated 2026-10-01 from a review of 370 essays (2026-07-05 → 2026-10-01) on one public Pattaya expat blog, read in full by four readers against CLAUDE.md, the canon layers already ingested and `docs/guardrails.md`. **This ledger is a reading list and a work queue, not a source of text**: every essay is used as RESEARCH under `docs/source-material-policy.md` — the social mechanic, the archetype, the price, the schedule — never its prose, its dialogue, its characters or its storyline with the names changed. The `thesis` and `proposal` columns are the reviewers' own paraphrases.

**Counts.** By category: MECHANIC 83 · STORY 181 · BUILT 39 · TONE 43 · SKIP 24. By usefulness (1–5): 5 → 14, 4 → 98, 3 → 150, 2 → 80, 1 → 28. 24 essays are flagged `guardrail` (they name real people or venues — nothing in them may be carried over as written).

**Category key.** BUILT — the game already has it (the row says what, if anything, the essay adds). MECHANIC — a rule, meter, modal, price, schedule or ending the game could carry. STORY — an NPC, arc, register or Owl-column subject. TONE — background only. SKIP — off-topic, duplicate, or a guardrail risk.

## Themes that recur across many essays (one mechanic each, not one per essay)

1. **The obligation economy** (bunkhun, kreng jai, tob taen, nam jai — ~10 essays). Gifts and favours open accounts in both directions: a big gift from the wrong man is a debt she may refuse, repayment comes later and a little bigger, and the her-farang tier is where obligation STARTS, not where it ends. Today `SEND`/`TIP` raise favour in proportion to the amount, and her-farang is pure upside (the waived barfine). Tan already models the favours half.
2. **Cheap care beats money** (how Thai men court, the anchored usual, silent remediation, the som tam in silence). Presence acts — seeing her home at closing, remembering her drink, mookata with her friends — as a bond route that outranks lady drinks. The presence bonus in `_endNight` is the seed.
3. **Bonds that survive the flight** (the invisible transition, the regular as a retirement plan, one more year, the quiet return). The most consistent claim in the corpus is that her decisive moment is his RETURN; the game resets bond, ledger and rep every vacation, so a returning man is a stranger. Carry a her-farang bond across NEW VACATION at a reduced tier, with texts between trips.
4. **Her phone as a second life** (8+ essays: the shadow profile, the 2 a.m. crop, the recurring ghost, the phantom funeral, the copy-paste reply, the alibi video call, the sad mor lam post). One system on top of the contacts code: money-asks with memory (the same uncle dies twice), day-stable absences that are true, selfies whose register changes with tier, asks weighted by what you have sent and how long since you met. The shelved black-square note is one piece of it.
5. **Collisions and sightings** (6 essays). A known woman appears in a room you did not expect — with another farang, in daylight, at the mall — placed by pure hash, prose only, and you choose between raising your glass and going over. The streak/collision seed in the long-term-play note.
6. **Face-loss is permanent** (you no good man, the rooftop, just sanuk). A public scene in front of a bonded woman or her kin sets a verdict money cannot reverse; the women's LINE group is how it travels. The game has no souring that cannot be bought back.
7. **The Thai man who pays in face** (the doorman who was her boyfriend, the man waiting on the bike outside the hotel, the branch manager, the shadow husband — 7 essays). A Thai-side social layer the game names in the long-term note and has not built; canon to confirm: Thai men are not customers in farang bars.
8. **Scheduled absences that are true** (the November harvest, the desant to Bangkok in the trough, a grandmother's funeral, alternating childcare nights). `_npcActive` absences with a story, answered by colleagues. CLAUDE.md says the staff never thin; the corpus says they do, at the harvest and in the wet.
9. **The house's filter is failing** (the algorithm as mamasan, the Tinder fallacy, the sideline student, the phone girl). Apps and digital sponsors pull attention and contacts off the bar; on the owner side it shows as leaked takings; CONTACT would be covert, because mamasans fine girls for giving numbers without a barfine.
10. **Market fracture and the bar that dies** (the bar that closed next door, the market fracture, the premium bottleneck). The script for `docs/bar-failure-cycle.md`: rent rises when the landlord sees a busy rail, the owner's price lever, the girls leaving because THEIR takings fell, the silent loans, the sign — watched from the bar opposite.
11. **Exits the women make themselves** (the successful exit, the reverse escape, once a bar girl, the border filter). Some filler women carry a stated target; a later vacation finds them gone — to a laundry, a salon, a coffee shop — and the town says where.
12. **Go-go badge numbers** (number 42, the gravity of 148, the som tum deflection). A hash badge on go-go dancers, targetable (`BARFINE 42`), with a bond-tier line about the number as armour.

## Where the essays say the game is wrong

- **Bonds reset per vacation** — the corpus's most consistent claim is that coming back is the moment (theme 3).
- **Rent is flat** — `_barRent()` never moves; the landlord raises it when he sees success and never really lowers it (theme 10).
- **Staff never thin** — they do: the harvest in November, Bangkok in the trough (theme 8). November is the game's "high" season, so the absence lands in the busy month.
- **A big gift always helps** — a gift from a man she does not want to owe is a debt she may refuse (theme 1).
- **The her-farang waiver is pure upside** — the source material's most common collision is exactly there: the loyal regular is still a subscriber, skipping the fine costs her salary, and she expects him to cover it (themes 1, 2).
- **A lady drink always pays out** — several essays: a drink often buys only the drink.
- **CONTACT is free and open** — it is covert in the sources; mamasans fine for it.
- **Jealousy is about her bar** — the game docks bond for barfining a colleague in front of her; the sources say colleagues do not bother her and an unbought civilian woman does.
- **Tragic backstories are de-duplicated per bar** — the public-template essay argues identical tragedies are the realistic case; repeats across town should read as true.
- **Laundries are refused non-doors** — in one essay the laundry IS the happy ending.
- **Thai men on a farang rail** — the sources state local men cannot be customers in these bars; worth confirming as canon so the game never seats one.
- **The owner has no lever on his own floor** — the bar's asset is the women and a departing girl takes her regulars with her, but nothing an owner decides moves `BAR_QUOTA`/`BAR_SALARY`, and the floor thins only on arrears (the 2026-10-01 essay; theme 10).
